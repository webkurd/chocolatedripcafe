Our Story

Born from a love of pure indulgence

"Each visit has left me at a new level of dessert euphoria."

Chocolate Drip is Richardson's most talked-about café — a destination where handcrafted desserts meet warm, attentive service and a setting that feels worlds apart from the ordinary.

From our famous pistachio croissants to our theatrical Dubai Chocolate Cups, every item on our menu is crafted to create a moment worth remembering. We believe indulgence is a feeling, and we pour it into everything we do.
